# Locked Project Scope

## Final Project Title
Risk-Aware Credit Card Growth Decision Engine

## One-line Positioning
A Capital One-style customer strategy simulator for offer targeting, risk-adjusted profitability, responsible-lending guardrails, scenario simulation, and A/B test planning.

## Core Business Question
Which credit card customers should receive which growth offer, what is the expected business impact, and which offers should be scaled, tested, or blocked due to credit risk?

## Must Include
- Customer segmentation
- Risk score or default probability proxy
- Risk-adjusted profitability
- Next-best-action recommendation engine
- Responsible-lending guardrails
- Scenario simulator
- A/B test planner
- Executive decision memo
- Streamlit dashboard

## Do Not Drift Into
- Generic bank dashboard
- Pure credit default prediction model
- Another public company finance dashboard
- Overly complex ML with weak business recommendation
- Capital One internal/confidential claims
